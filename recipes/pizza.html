Description

Pizza is a popular Italian dish made with a round dough base topped with tomato sauce and cheese.

It can also include different toppings such as vegetables, meat, and other ingredients. It is baked in the oven until the crust becomes golden and the cheese melts.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Onion
  • Tomato
  • Bell pepper
  • Olive oil

Steps to prepare pizza

  1. Preheat the oven.
  2. Prepare the pizza dough and shape it into a round base.
  3. Spread tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  4. Add mozzarella cheese on top of the sauce.
  5. Add onion, tomato, and bell pepper as toppings.
  6. Place the pizza in the oven and bake until the crust is golden.
  7. Remove the pizza from the oven, let it cool slightly, and serve.
