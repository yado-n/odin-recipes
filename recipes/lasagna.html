Description

Lasagna is a traditional Italian dish made with layers of pasta, meat sauce, and cheese.

It is baked in the oven until the cheese becomes golden and the layers are hot and delicious.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna pasta sheets
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Onion
  • garlic
  • cheese

Steps to prepare lasagna

  1. open oven medium heat
  2. chop onion and garlic cook them
  3. Add meat and cook the add tomato sauce
  4. when sauce cooked well prepare the lasagna pasta
  5. on oven plate place lasagna layer by layer with sauce and cheese
  6. Put oven and Bake lasagna
